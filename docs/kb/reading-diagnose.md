The **Diagnose** tab answers one question: *what should I look at first?* It is a
single ranked queue of **levers** — one honest list, tuning and behavioral
mixed, most worth-your-attention at the top.

## The rank

Each row is scored by `∛(severity × recurrence × confidence)` — how bad, how
often, how sure. A big score means a lever that hurt a lot, happened repeatedly,
and the engine is confident about. The equation sits behind the per-lever
expander; the factor bars on the card show you the three inputs at a glance.

## Setting vs. habit

Every lever is chipped **⚙ Setting** or **◈ Habit**. That flavor is a chip, never
a lane — the rank stays honest across both. What differs is where "accept" sends
you:

- **⚙ Setting** findings (basal, ISF, or I:C) flow to
  [The Plan tab](#the-plan-tab) as a proposed settings change.
- **◈ Habit** levers (late bolus, over-treated low) flow to Focus / Verify, because
  no pump setting fixes them.

## "Why so few?"

A short queue is a *good* sign, not a broken one. The tail of the surface tells
you how many opportunities the engine looked at and chose to stay quiet on, and
why — thin windows, upstream causes, near-misses under threshold. Read it before
you assume nothing's wrong.
