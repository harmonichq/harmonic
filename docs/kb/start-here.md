This tool reads *your own* Control-IQ history and tells you where your basal,
ISF, and I:C settings might be off — and where your habits, not your settings,
are driving the highs and lows. It is **advisory**. It never touches your pump;
it hands you a specific number and the evidence behind it to take to your
clinician.

## How to read any verdict here

Every claim carries an **evidence tier**, and the tier is the first thing to
read:

- **Observed** — a hard fact from the feed (a 4.5 U bolus at 19:15, BG hitting
  58). Trust it plainly.
- **Inferred** — derived from the *shape* of the curve, so it is hedged
  ("likely rescue carbs"). Weigh it, don't bank on it.
- **Not in data** — an explicit unknown the feed can't show, flagged rather than
  guessed.

The engine would rather stay silent than assert something the feed can't
support. **Silence is a verdict**, not a gap — see [How the engine reasons](#the-pipeline).
