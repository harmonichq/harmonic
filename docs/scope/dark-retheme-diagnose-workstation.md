# Dark Diagnose retheme — triage and review ledger

Ticket: #255

## Decisions

- Treat the operator-approved issue prototype as a settled visual specification,
  not as source code to copy from the control checkout.
- Route the shipped surface through UI Craft `revise` and preserve its frozen
  behavior ledger and replay unchanged.
- Split delivery into three dependent capabilities: production visual grammar,
  generated/browser contract parity, and no-fetch built-app evidence with scoped
  reconciliation.
- Keep Light, mobile Day hero, dock-floor redesign, new hold/no-data treatment,
  analyzer/API behavior, fixtures, and real-data evidence out of scope.
- Preserve the chart protections merged by #256, including plot-only gates,
  selection-independent basal paint, passive-state tells, and final-pixel
  legibility.

## Risk contract

- Must prevent: changed dosing guidance or staging behavior; hidden evidence,
  focus, state, or chart furniture; weakened #253/#256 gates; stale extracts; or
  patient-derived evidence.
- Must recover: no new fallback. Existing theme repaint, chart builders, and
  interaction owners remain authoritative.
- Accepted failure: absent browser dependencies, generated synthetic database,
  or vendored assets fail closed and require manual environment repair.
- Unsupported: any item named out of scope above, or a visual correction outside
  the closed expected diff.
- Evidence owed: fail-first source/extract and rendered assertions; unchanged
  behavior replay; affected browser and drift gates; and inspected same-fixture
  Dark base/revision captures.
