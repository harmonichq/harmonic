BASAL
Basal rateU/h
The continuous background insulin Control-IQ delivers between meals and corrections.
Programmed basalU/h
The rate set in your pump's profile for a given time of day.
Delivered basalU/h
What Control-IQ actually delivered, which can differ from the programmed rate when the algorithm adjusts up, down, or suspends.
Clean window
A stretch of time used as evidence because it wasn't disturbed by a recent bolus, meal, or algorithm correction — so the delivered basal reflects the profile setting, not a reaction to something else.
CORRECTION FACTOR
Correction factorISF · mg/dL per U — Insulin Sensitivity Factor
How much one unit of insulin is expected to lower blood glucose. A lower number means more insulin is needed to correct the same rise.
Wide CI
A confidence interval (see CI below) wide enough that the estimate is uncertain — flagged so you know to weight it less.
CARB RATIO
Carb ratioI:C · g/U — Insulin-to-Carb ratio
How many grams of carbohydrate one unit of insulin covers. A lower number means more insulin is needed per gram of carbs.
GENERAL
CI
Confidence interval — the range an estimate is likely to fall within given the data available. Narrower means more reliable.
Epoch
A stretch over which a pump setting appears unchanged. Basal setting epochs can narrow basal measurement windows; ISF/I:C measurements use the full requested window and keep setting epochs as caveats/settling context.
CGM
Continuous Glucose Monitor — the sensor providing the glucose readings shown throughout this app.
BolusU
A single dose of insulin given for a meal or to correct a high, as opposed to the continuous basal rate.
IOB
Insulin On Board — insulin already delivered that is still active in the body.
Advisory only — review with your clinician before changing pump settings.
