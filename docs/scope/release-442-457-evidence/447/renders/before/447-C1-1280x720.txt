Reading the Diagnose surface

Written for the v1 tabs: Diagnose keeps its name here, Plan reads as Changes.

The Diagnose tab answers one question: what should I look at first? It is a single ranked queue of levers — one honest list, tuning and behavioral mixed, most worth-your-attention at the top.

THE RANK

Each row is scored by ∛(severity × recurrence × confidence) — how bad, how often, how sure. A big score means a lever that hurt a lot, happened repeatedly, and the engine is confident about. The equation sits behind the per-lever expander; the factor bars on the card show you the three inputs at a glance.

SETTING VS. HABIT

Every lever is chipped ⚙ Setting or ◈ Cause. That flavor is a chip, never a lane — the rank stays honest across both. What differs is where "accept" sends you:

⚙ Setting findings (basal, ISF, or I:C) flow to The Plan tab as a proposed settings change.
◈ Cause levers (late bolus, over-treated low) flow to Focus / Verify, because no pump setting fixes them.
"WHY SO FEW?"

A short queue is a good sign, not a broken one. The tail of the surface tells you how many opportunities the engine looked at and chose to stay quiet on, and why — thin windows, upstream causes, near-misses under threshold. Read it before you assume nothing's wrong.

Open your Diagnose surface → (Diagnose here) to see your own ranked levers.

Advisory only — review with your clinician before changing pump settings.
