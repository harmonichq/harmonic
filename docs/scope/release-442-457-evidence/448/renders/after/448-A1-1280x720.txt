The silence taxonomy: why the engine stays quiet
OBSERVED

No trigger The behavior plainly didn't happen: a flat slope, no stack, no correction, no low. A genuinely clean opportunity.

Under threshold It happened but fell short of the firing bar: the near-miss where a mis-tuned threshold hides.

Prior high baseline The rise started from an already-high level, not from flat, so it isn't the from-flat run this detector looks for.

Horizon expired The outcome never arrived inside the detector's window. The story ran past the edge of what it watches.

INFERRED

Upstream cause An observable recent low and/or a defensive suspend explains the move, or the rise is the rebound of an over-treated low, which owns it. A recovery, not the behavior itself.

Owned by prior bolus A completed carb bolus in the recent lookback already owns the rise this dose landed into. A real excursion already covered, not a late meal bolus.

Owned by announced meal A substantial announced meal at the low owns the rebound, so fast-carb treatment cannot be isolated from the meal response.

NOT IN DATA

Insufficient data The window was too sparse to judge, with not enough readings to say anything honest.
