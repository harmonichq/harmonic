Clock window — unrolling past midnight

The window is start + duration (mod 24h). Drag on the strip to select one. The chart is full width at rest and never rescales; once the drag edge reaches either end of the day the day slides underneath it and the neighbouring day's hours arrive from the far side, so the edge stays exactly under the cursor. It works both ways — right past 24:00, or left past 00:00. Keep going and the drag runs a full day, landing back on its own start as the whole-day scope. Release and the axis rolls back to 0–24, where a wrapped window renders as two bands under one label.

Where the limits sit. A drag runs in either direction until it meets its own start, one full day out, and lands there on the whole day — the scope the backend already expresses as an unwindowed day, so the gesture has no dead end and no near-miss. Presets stay contiguous; a wrapped window is something you draw, not something you pick. The wrap exists only in committed state: during the gesture the axis is monotonic, which is why the cursor never detaches from the edge it is dragging.
