Direction B, “Glass Cockpit”

Avionics primary-flight-display idiom. The glucose-by-clock strip is the horizon instrument: the slice window is a bright gate on a dimmed 24-hour band, and the window’s own statistics are etched into the strip’s right margin rather than floated in a panel. Beneath it, evidence charts sit edge to edge, separated only by hairlines — no cards, no borders, no shadows; each chart carries an engraved label at top-left and its by-clock / by-event switch at top-right. Pins are bright tick brackets at the chart’s corners; layout is always derived from pin state. Findings mode adds a thin amber rank filament along the seated chart’s top edge; Explore mode extinguishes every advisory colour. All four frames are 1440×900 and draw one committed fixture set — the Diagnose workstation payload, its Correction factor rest-window capture and its Carb ratio block-evidence capture. One exception: the Correction factor chart’s delta-BG values are invented for legibility under a fixed seed, because the committed capture holds every reading at a single glucose value and so carries no spread to draw.
