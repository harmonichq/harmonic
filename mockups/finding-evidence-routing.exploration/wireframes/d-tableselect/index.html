Wireframe D — the table is the selector

No control above the list. All 18 comparable events stay on the page at all times, grouped factor → cohort; the group headers are the scope handles. Clicking a header focuses that group and the room dims around it. Clicking it again releases. Grayscale, system font, hairlines — deliberately not the app.

State 1 — nothing focused

All groups at equal ink. The chart names the whole population.

State 2 — a factor focused

“Over-treated low” clicked. Its rows hold full ink; every other group recedes to 35% but keeps its place, its counts and its row heights. Nothing collapses and nothing moves.

State 3 — a cohort focused inside a focused factor

Second click depth: “Near rule” inside Unclaimed. The other cohorts of that factor recede too, so the dim is two-deep — the craft risk this state exists to test.
