Direction E — the collapsing selector

At rest the factor selector is one line that reads as a title: current factor, its count, a quiet chevron. Tapping expands it in place into the whole factor list; one tap switches and it collapses again. Tap-away or Esc collapses. The comparison chart always draws all three states — selection changes which factor is being asked about, never which cohorts are drawn.
