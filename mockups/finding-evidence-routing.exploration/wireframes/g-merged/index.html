Wireframe G — merged direction

Selection lives entirely in the inspector: a collapsing factor dropdown at the column head (from E), over the denser event ledger (from F). The canvas head carries exactly one control — an alignment projection switch, By clock / By event — which reprojects the already-selected data and never selects it. Within a factor the canvas always draws all three states; there is no cohort filter.

Push vs overlay. The expanded list overlays the ledger rather than pushing it. Pushing an 8-row / 352px list down the column would drive the first event rows past the fold and move the dock, so a mis-tap on the factor name costs the reader their place in the table. Overlaying leaves the ledger and the floor dock exactly where they were, so collapsing restores the identical column — and at 8 factors the expanded list is 8 × 44px + header ≈ 400px inside a ~730px column, still one screen with the table's first rows visible beneath it. Tap-away (the scrim), Esc, or choosing a factor all collapse it.
