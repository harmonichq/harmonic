Reading a Day

Written for the v1 tabs: Diagnose reads as Explore here, Plan as Changes.

The Day tab is the forensic replay: I crashed on Tuesday — what happened? It pairs one day's glucose chart with a chronological Episode Log.

THE CHART

The sticky glucose curve runs the width of the day, banded by target range. Boluses, carbs, and basal changes are marked on the timeline — every color is paired with a shape or label so the marks read without relying on color alone.

THE EPISODE LOG

Down the right, each episode the engine assembled is a card in time order: an anchor (a meal bolus, a low, an unexplained rise), the window around it, and the one lever attributed to it — its earliest actionable driver. Behaviors that also fired are folded in as consequences of that lever, never stacked as separate blame.

Click an episode to highlight its window on the chart above. That handoff — log row ↔ chart region — is how you read why a moment went the way it did.
