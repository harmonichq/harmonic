# Design — basal evidence goes editorial

## ADR 205 — One editorial treatment, deltas from the programmed rule

The basal tile's form was settled by an attended review loop on the operator's real history: independent chart-designer directions were built as competing harness modes, swept for text collisions across every real slot plus manufactured edge payloads, and converged by operator rulings. The rulings that bind the form: nights are independent observations, so no mark may span more than one night and no shared baseline may join them; each night is a delta anchored on its own served programmed rate — the drawn rule is today's rate, and a roster spanning a profile change renders nights off the rule honestly — with direction taken from the served per-night sign, never re-derived from geometry (direction is a side, not a remembered color); the reader picked the slot, so copy states findings without scene-setting, and every fact prints in exactly one home; the y-axis is a stack of individual occurrences. The clock/event toggle is retired — one verdict, one treatment; the count that argues against the finding (excluded nights) may never sit deeper than the claim it qualifies.

## ADR 205 — Seat rank follows measured width, not seat name

The tile adapts by the host's measured width (threshold 780px): full furniture, a middle rank keeping the figure plus compressed verdict and tally lines, and the dock mini silhouette. The seat name was rejected as the seam because a narrowed focal stage needs the same protection as an explorer cell; the box is the fact, the seat is only an intention.

## ADR 205 — The night-evidence payload carries the verdict triad as copies

`basal_night_evidence.project` copies `current`, `recommended`, and `estimate` from the analyzer row, deriving nothing. The chart draws the rule, estimate, and interval from served facts, keeping the one-predicate staging invariant untouched; the projection module's copies-only contract is unchanged in kind.

## ADR 205 — Ledger stories identify charts by identity, not by axis-shape proxies

S102 asserted the shared glucose range over every chart whose y-axis had finite bounds — a proxy the old basal chart escaped by accident of an auto minimum. The amended story excludes the basal tile by chart id. A ledger assertion that selects its subjects by a rendering accident breaks on any honest redesign; identity is the durable selector.
