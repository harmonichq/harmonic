# Proposal — basal evidence goes editorial

## Why

The basal night-evidence chart drew two connected lines over independent nights, asserting a continuity the data does not have, and its event mode rendered one number as a bar. Reviewed live against the operator's own history (#205), neither surface answered the question the slot exists to answer: did the algorithm have to deliver more than the programmed rate, and does the evidence justify the recommendation?

## What changes

- The basal tile becomes a single editorial treatment: a factual headline, one delta cell per night anchored on the programmed rule (sorted, no mark spanning more than one night), the interval as a wordless shadow on the rate axis, a typeset verdict rail, and honest denominators including the excluded count.
- The clock/event mode toggle is retired; event-aligned basal rows resolve to the same treatment.
- The tile carries three width-ranked seats: full, a measured-width middle rank for the explorer grid, and the dock mini silhouette.
- `/api/diagnose/basal-night-evidence` copies the analyzer row's `current`, `recommended`, and `estimate` (value, lo, hi, confidence) so the chart draws the verdict apparatus from served facts (#290's triad half).
- The harness basal story gains slot selection; behavior-ledger story S102 identifies glucose axes by chart identity instead of a finite-bounds proxy.

## Boundaries

No analyzer, safety predicate, staging verdict, or findings-queue change. The chart renames backend verdicts and never re-derives one. The exclusion-reason breakdown and inspector rebuild stay with #290/#291.
