# Design — chrome-bar-surface-states

## ADR 49 — Current and hover are different chrome states

**Ruling.** The current workflow step is an outline-and-disc component with no plate fill. The theme-menu hover row uses a neutral lift mixed from 95% `--ck-panel` and 5% `--ck-meta`, never `--ck-accent-soft`. The Harmonic mark and favicon remain unchanged.

### Context

ADR 47 derived `--ck-bar-signal` and `--ck-bar-signal-well` against the chrome bar's own grounds. Its arithmetic also proved that the current-step plate has an empty band: a fill cannot sit 3:1 under the signal disc and 3:1 above the bar at once. The shipped component is conforming because its border, rather than its fill, carries the boundary.

The same well is also painted under a transient theme-menu hover. That hover carries no persistent state, while the checked theme is already expressed by orange text and `aria-checked`. Reusing the orange well makes hover resemble selection and spends the interaction signal on a state that does not need it.

### Decision

Remove the current-step background. Keep its existing one-pixel signal-derived outline, bright label, and filled number disc. Those three existing shapes express the current position without adding a token or moving the disc/well contrast problem elsewhere. The change is visual only and must not alter button geometry.

Replace the menu hover background with:

```css
color-mix(in srgb, var(--ck-panel) 95%, var(--ck-meta))
```

This is a neutral surface lift, not a semantic signal. The checked row continues to own the orange ink. Do not introduce a `--ck-hover` token for this single caller.

### Candidate check and acceptance authority

The candidate was rendered in the safe local app using the exact declared command `uv run harmonic serve --no-fetch --db mockups/revise-e2e.synthetic/harmonic.sqlite`; that committed database is synthetic and generated by `scripts/gen_revise_e2e_db.py`. An exploratory Chromium probe cleared the required floors, but its rounded values are not acceptance targets: computed floating-point colours and rasterized 8-bit samples round differently.

The implementation's browser assertions over computed painted styles are the authority. The current-step outline and number disc must each clear 3:1 against the bar ground; the current-step label, checked theme ink, and unchecked row ink must each clear 4.5:1 against their painted backgrounds; and the focus outline must retain its existing 3:1 floor. The neutral hover fill itself carries no 3:1 requirement because it is transient and conveys no otherwise-hidden state. It must be visibly distinct from the panel and from the orange signal well without weakening either row ink.

### Locked constraints

- `--ck-bar-signal`, `--ck-bar-signal-well`, and `--ck-bar-on-signal` do not change.
- The current-step button retains its size, padding, radius, outline, label, number disc, and focus-visible treatment.
- The theme menu retains its size, row geometry, checked-state ink, roles, and selection behavior.
- `.cockpit-mark` and `frontend/favicon.svg` remain byte-for-byte unchanged; #736 and ADR 47 still govern the identity object.
- Before implementation, freeze the cockpit shell's behavior ledger against the base app and its synthetic database. ADR 49 authorizes exactly two changed stories—current-step treatment and theme-menu hover treatment—which receive dated amendments and old-fail/new-pass evidence. No behavior retirement is authorized.

### Rejected directions

- **Lift the current-step plate until it clears 3:1 against the bar.** This forces a new ink/boundary treatment inside the plate and turns a compact workflow marker into a heavy filled control.
- **Keep the orange hover well.** It preserves the selection/hover ambiguity that opened this design decision.
- **Use the sheet's neutral or signal tokens.** The bar owns its own ground and interaction vocabulary under ADR 47.
- **Retune the identity mark.** It is an exempt, locked brand object, not an interaction surface.

Decision: harmonichq/harmonic#49, 2026-08-20.
