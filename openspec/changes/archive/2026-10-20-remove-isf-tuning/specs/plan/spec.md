## REMOVED Requirements

### Requirement: Direction-only ISF recommendations cannot be staged

**Reason:** ADR 564: Harmonic does not estimate or tune the correction factor, because no passive method identifies it under Control-IQ.

**Migration:** None. A recorded ISF Plan still reads and keeps its subject name; no new one can be recorded.

### Requirement: A stageable fasting ISF applies to every programmed ISF segment

**Reason:** ADR 564: Harmonic does not estimate or tune the correction factor, because no passive method identifies it under Control-IQ.

**Migration:** None. A recorded ISF Plan still reads and keeps its subject name; no new one can be recorded.
