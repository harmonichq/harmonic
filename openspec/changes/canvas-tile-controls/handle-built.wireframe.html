The handle, built

Execution, not options. Ruled and closed: knurled tab · knurl centred · no word · pane-fill glyphs · left shrinks, right grows · a control is absent where it cannot act. The earlier rounds were sketches and read like it; this is the same object drawn with the plate edges, the light direction and the optical grid the rest of the surface uses.

Every dimension is one variable scaled by --u, so the 3× and 1× specimens are the same object and cannot drift apart. The knurl is milled rather than printed: each ridge is a dark line with a lit line beside it, so the texture has a light direction. At 1× that is one pixel of each, which is what a knurl actually looks like at this size.

The glyphs

one square · filled by how much of the pane the dock takes

Mount and shrink differ by how much of the square is filled, not by a mirrored direction — the failure every other candidate shared, since opposed acts drawn as mirrors read as each other at 13px. Bring-up and shrink are deliberately the same mark: they have the same destination, the docked row.
