Rank, not hue

A role owns a rank, not a colour. Rank 1 is the tile the inspector is holding; rank 2 is a held control — a pin, the live alignment cell; rank 3 is everything else in the rail. Each ground then spends whichever hue actually reaches that rung against it: terracotta over bone, bone-white over graphite. The themes agree about the order and disagree about the paint.

Both grounds are live below, side by side, tokens scoped per field so the ordering is compared rather than remembered. Correction factor is drilled; Basal is pinned; nothing is hovered. The table under each field measures what the eye is being asked to agree with — rank 1 has to out-contrast rank 2 on its own ground, or the rule is only a story.
