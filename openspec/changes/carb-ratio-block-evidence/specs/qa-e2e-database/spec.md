## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A carb-ratio block evidence era proves the served explainability facts exactly

The QA catalog SHALL carry a manufactured `ic-block-evidence` case: two programmed
carb-ratio blocks; chained runs shared across them; excluded runs of every served
pool reason; one pooled chain that spikes above range and later prints an
attributed low; at least two attributed lows on separate days; and a block that
asserts a raise. Its expectation SHALL be literal dumps of the served analyzer,
queue, support, run-reason, ledger, outcome and harm rows, never derived at
assertion time, and the case SHALL leave the committed showcase unchanged and
every recorded budget within its limit.

#### Scenario: The case store serves every reason and both lows

- **WHEN** the `ic-block-evidence` case is materialized and served through the
  production endpoints
- **THEN** its block evidence carries a run under each served pool reason, a
  tally with ran-high and ran-low counts, and two attributed lows on separate
  days, matching the literal expectation exactly
