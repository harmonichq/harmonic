# Promote the Diagnose dark retheme (#255)

## Why

The shipped Diagnose Dark surface collapses its pane bodies, chart fields,
vessels, and rails into a narrow grey-brown band. Chart grounds can read lighter
than the panes that contain them, edges disappear into gridline ink, and the
filled glucose target band becomes a slab across the plot. The result weakens
the visual hierarchy of advisory evidence even though the underlying evidence
and interactions remain correct.

Issue #255 records the operator-approved correction: one warm tonal ladder,
distinct material roles, one chart-vessel edge, shadow-only spotlight emphasis,
target rails instead of a filled band, and quieter overlapping meal-run strokes.

## What changes

- Re-settle the affected terms of the locked Harmonic Dark theme and record each
  rescinded rule in this change's design record.
- Derive the Dark app, chart, cockpit, and compatibility-token namespaces from
  one material ladder while leaving Light unchanged.
- Give chart vessels one visible edge treatment, make wells darker than sheets,
  and make spotlight elevation shadow-only.
- Replace the filled 70–180 mg/dL target band with dashed boundary rails in the
  named Diagnose evidence charts and the existing Day chart builder.
- Reduce overlapping carb-ratio run opacity so individual strands remain
  readable.
- Bind the synthetic evidence-canvas exploration to the shipped theme sources,
  regenerate it, and preserve every existing fail-closed drift gate.
- Prove the revision against the shipped behavior replay, the affected browser
  gates, and same-fixture Dark before/after renders.

## Risk contract

- **Must prevent:** a Dark retheme that changes Light; hides advisory evidence,
  focus, state, or chart furniture; weakens the final-pixel protections landed by
  #253/#256; changes findings, chart data, staging, safety, or interaction
  semantics; leaves a committed extract stale; or publishes patient-derived
  evidence.
- **Must recover:** nothing automatically. Theme repaint, chart construction,
  and interaction remain on their existing owners.
- **Accepted failure:** missing browser prerequisites, vendored assets, or the
  generated synthetic database stop the evidence run loudly and require manual
  environment repair.
- **Unsupported:** changing the Light theme; resolving the dock-floor treatment;
  redesigning hold/no-data states or the hero glucose chart; changing the mobile
  Day hero builder; or changing any analyzer, recommendation, API, fixture value,
  or real-health-data workflow.
- **Evidence owed:** fail-first chart-option and rendered-style assertions; the
  complete Diagnose behavior replay; affected Day, Diagnose, canvas-composition,
  and cockpit browser gates; both theme-extract drift checks; the repository fast
  gate; and inspected synthetic Dark before/after renders.

## Impact

The shipped Dark theme, Diagnose workstation presentation, two existing chart
builders, their public tests, the frozen surface ledger/replay as a preservation
contract, the synthetic evidence-canvas extract, the `surfaces` capability, and
this change record. No backend or advisory computation changes.
