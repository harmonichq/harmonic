## Design brief

Job: choose a clock window, compare findings, inspect the selected evidence.
Audience: the pump wearer reading their own history, often with limited attention.
Direction: the selected evidence leads; the queue is a consistent list.
Signature move: selection changes emphasis without changing row geometry.
Density: dense queue, generous evidence area, smaller window overview.
Constraints: preserve information and backend-owned judgments; existing dark
material; keyboard-operable wireframe controls; narrow screen alternative.
Anti-references: raised first-row card, misaligned type tags, duplicate headlines.

## First round — arrangement

A. Evidence first (recommended): queue remains right, selected evidence starts
immediately below window controls, overview below the evidence. Cost: precise
window slicing is farther from the presets; a top "Adjust window" shortcut
must bring it into view without hiding the active range.

B. Findings left: same evidence and queue, navigation reads left to right;
overview spans the bottom. Cost: reverses the learned arrangement and reduces
vertical room for evidence on short screens.

C. Compact overview: a shallow overview stays above both panes and expands
in place for slicing. Cost: another expansion step and evidence moves down
while the overview is expanded.

Rank, type, denominator and selected-state columns keep common positions.
Thumbnails are explicitly schematic placeholders. No single row becomes a card.
Active scope stays at the top for every arrangement.

The footer calls out interaction boundaries. No approve/lock button is offered.
