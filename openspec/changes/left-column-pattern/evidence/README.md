# Evidence — the left-column pattern (#306)

Every artifact here is synthetic. Renders and replays read the committed
Diagnose payload (`mockups/diagnose-workstation.synthetic/payload.json`,
generated by `.claude/qa/gen_synthetic_fixtures.py` from a fixed seed) through
the declared no-fetch server, each checkout served from its own scratch copy of
`mockups/qa-e2e.synthetic/harmonic.sqlite` (generated in full by
`scripts/gen_qa_e2e_db.py`). The facts sheets read the served showcase and four
named QA case stores emitted by `scripts/gen_qa_e2e_db.py --case`. No real
patient data is read or shown anywhere in this directory.

## Headlines (sub-order 1)

- `headline-facts.py` — the generator: every findings row across every window
  preset plus the whole-day read, with every served field and the evidence its
  chart reads; `ref_` columns are reference-only (Finding case file) and never
  template slots. Run against served stores; no `--check` (needs a served app;
  exemption recorded in `design.md`).
- `headline-facts.csv` — its output over `showcase`,
  `basal-insufficient-seven-night`, `isf-strengthen`, `ic-raise`, `ic-held`.
- `headlines.authored.csv` — the operator-ruled templates rendered against
  every row through the projection's own headline function, verdict first.

## Renders

`render-states.mjs` opens one named state from one checkout through that
checkout's own replay opener (`openApp` with `frontendRoot`), so a base render
serves the base frontend and an after render serves the revision's. States:
`queue-root`, `drill-basal`, `drill-carb-ratio`, `drill-correction-factor`,
`drill-finding` (all drills from the Morning preset, the one committed window
that ranks every family), `watching-promoted`, `drawer-hidden`, `drawer-up`,
`explorer`; viewports 1440×900, 1280×800 and 390×844. Files under `renders/`
are `before-<state>-<w>x<h>.png` (base, port 8307) and
`after-<state>-<w>x<h>.png` (revision, port 8308). At 390×844 the stage is
collapsed on base and revision alike, so the narrow pair shows the queue and
the drawer only.

What the pairs show: the base boots the drawer docked and the revision boots it
hidden (`queue-root`, `drawer-hidden`); the base draws the basal headline inside
the chart under a micro-caps nameplate, and the revision's stage card carries
the slot name as a label with the served verdict sentence as its title and the
measured facts as its subtitle (`drill-basal`, `drill-carb-ratio`,
`drill-correction-factor`, `drill-finding`); a Watching promotion puts the
drawer away on the revision (`watching-promoted`).
