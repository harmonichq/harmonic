# Retire the legacy basal ribbon and revise its queue noun (#104)

## Why

The shell still carried a complete basal coverage-ribbon screen that the shipped
app could not place on the page. Its state, chart builders, styles, and evidence
strips had no template binding, route, or URL entry point, but their presence made
the retired surface look available to future work.

Separately, a shipped findings-queue row called its basal support denominator
“clean nights”. Harmonic's locked reader-facing term is “nights of steady data”.
The queue reads that noun from the server projection, so every frozen encoding of
the projection must move together without changing the number, ranking, staging
verdict, or advice.

## What changes

- Remove the unreachable basal tier, block-diff, coverage-ribbon, and evidence-
  strip cluster, including only its transitive chart-builder and stylesheet
  orphans.
- Add a closed source inventory that keeps the retired identifiers and selector
  family absent while preserving the separate live prompt-queue ribbon.
- Publish “nights of steady data” as the basal findings-queue support noun in the
  Python projection, fixture-only JavaScript mirror, generated frozen answers,
  and unit-test expectation.
- Retire the archived design study's obsolete noun-respelling map while preserving
  its title pass and generated object shape.
- Re-freeze the existing finding-to-evidence behavior record and retain paired
  Light/Dark before-and-after evidence of the queue row.

## Risk contract

- **Must prevent:** deleting source the running app can reach; changing any
  analyzer, safety predicate, recommendation, staging eligibility, Plan behavior,
  or wearer-facing advice beyond the locked noun; publishing real health data.
- **Must recover:** nothing automatically.
- **Accepted failure:** none for the deletion; an incomplete source inventory or
  a replay that cannot reach every frozen story fails the change rather than
  degrading.
- **Unsupported:** a wider shell dead-code sweep; restoring the coverage ribbon or
  evidence strips; changing the basal case-file and manufactured-demo wording
  owned by #102; changing engine-domain comments or docstrings; verification with
  real pump data or a fetch-enabled server.
- **Evidence owed:** a naturally red then green closed source inventory; the
  projection mirror identical to regenerated backend-frozen answers; equal,
  all-green base and revision replay counts; paired rendered queue evidence; the
  fast gate, drift checks, and public-tree checks green.

Why: the deletion is unreachable presentation source and the copy correction moves
no advisory number, but both sit beside the evidence a wearer uses to judge dosing
guidance.

Disposition: inline in this proposal and unchanged from the locked work order.

## Impact

The shipped app loses only unreachable frontend source. The visible findings queue
now reads `19 nights of steady data · 30 d run` for the frozen merged basal span.
No API shape, stored data, analyzer, safety rule, recommendation, staging verdict,
or Plan behavior changes.
